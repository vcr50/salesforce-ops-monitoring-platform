# SentinelFlow + Zentom AI — AppExchange Demo Video Script

This document details the video storyboard, visual actions, and spoken narration script for the SentinelFlow + Zentom AI 2-minute product demonstration video.

---

## Video Specifications
*   **Target Duration**: 2 Minutes (120 Seconds)
*   **Format**: 1080p MP4 (16:9 ratio) at 30/60 FPS
*   **Tone**: Enterprise-grade, secure, professional, and clear

---

## Script Flow & Storyboard

### Scene 1: Introduction & The Command Center Dashboard
*   **Duration**: 0:00 - 0:25 (25 Seconds)
*   **Visual Actions (Video)**:
    *   Start on a clean, professional intro screen with the logo: **SentinelFlow + Zentom AI**.
    *   Transition to the main Command Center dashboard view in Salesforce.
    *   Hover over the KPI cards: Org Health, AI Confidence, and Active Predictions.
    *   Click on the tooltip annotation next to the **Estimated Cost Savings** card to show the formula.
*   **Narration (Audio)**:
    *   *"Welcome to SentinelFlow + Zentom AI—the autonomous immune system for your Salesforce organization. Today, we're looking at the v1.2.0 Command Center, a real-time operations console designed to give enterprise teams complete visibility into their system health.*
    *   *Our streaming telemetry network feeds telemetry events directly to this dark-mode glassmorphic interface. Here, operators track real-time system metrics, MTTR performance, and estimated cost savings driven by our self-healing infrastructure."*
*   **Sync Timing**:
    *   `0:05` - Telemetry Dashboard renders.
    *   `0:15` - Operator hovers over the KPI cards.
    *   `0:20` - Savings formula tooltip pops open.

### Scene 2: Anomaly Ingestion & AI Prediction Explainability
*   **Duration**: 0:25 - 0:55 (30 Seconds)
*   **Visual Actions (Video)**:
    *   Animate a live prediction warning card appearing on the Traffic Board (simulating an integration timeout spike).
    *   Click the warning card to open the detail panel.
    *   Highlight the Natural Language explainability block and the scoring weights (Deployment event vs. Integration error).
*   **Narration (Audio)**:
    *   *"Let's watch what happens when an anomaly is detected. Zentom AI constantly processes system logs, flow faults, and deployment events.*
    *   *When a threat arises—such as a webhook latency spike—the AI classifies the incident and generates an active prediction card.*
    *   *Instead of abstract scores, operators receive natural-language explanations detailing exactly why the prediction was raised. We see here that a recent HubSpot deployment correlated with an integration latency spike, raising a 78% critical risk prediction."*
*   **Sync Timing**:
    *   `0:30` - Anomaly warning card fades in with a micro-animation.
    *   `0:40` - Detail panel slides open from the right.
    *   `0:48` - Highlight the scoring weights list.

### Scene 3: Governed Auto-Heal & The Guardian Gate
*   **Duration**: 0:55 - 1:25 (30 Seconds)
*   **Visual Actions (Video)**:
    *   Transition to the **Guardian Gate** approvals page.
    *   Show the pending approval card for the timeout incident.
    *   Click **Request Approval**. Highlight that the incident is safely locked and no automated action can run without verification.
    *   Click **Approve** as the administrator user.
*   **Narration (Audio)**:
    *   *"SentinelFlow is built on a foundation of safety. With Governed Auto-Heal, we ensure that no critical remediation occurs autonomously. High-risk actions are routed directly to the Guardian Gate approval queue.*
    *   *Here, operators review recommendations and approve them in a single click. Row-level locks and transactional rollbacks ensure that if a recovery step fails, the system safely reverts to its original state without interrupting business operations."*
*   **Sync Timing**:
    *   `1:00` - Navigate to the Guardian Gate queue.
    *   `1:10` - Click the "Request Approval" action button.
    *   `1:18` - Click "Approve" and show the status changing to "Approved".

### Scene 4: Keyset Pagination & Tamper-Proof GRC Audits
*   **Duration**: 1:25 - 1:45 (20 Seconds)
*   **Visual Actions (Video)**:
    *   Scroll through a long historical grid table of resolved incidents. Highlight the fast, infinite pagination load.
    *   Navigate to the `Sentinel_Audit_Log__c` list view. Scroll through rows displaying `Trace UUID` fields.
*   **Narration (Audio)**:
    *   *"To support large-scale enterprise environments, our keyset pagination engine allows you to navigate millions of historical records with compound cursors, avoiding platform query limits.*
    *   *For regulatory compliance, every event, approval override, and failure state is logged in an immutable, write-once GRC audit trail, providing complete documentation for SOX and SOC2 audits."*
*   **Sync Timing**:
    *   `1:30` - Scroll down the historical incidents grid, showing smooth loading.
    *   `1:38` - Open the Compliance Audit log page.

### Scene 5: Outro & Next Steps
*   **Duration**: 1:45 - 2:00 (15 Seconds)
*   **Visual Actions (Video)**:
    *   Return to the dashboard view.
    *   Fade out to the ending screen: **SentinelFlow + Zentom AI**.
    *   Display tagline: **Secure. Learn. Heal. Scale.**
    *   Add Call-to-Action: **Start your free developer sandbox trial today on the AppExchange.**
*   **Narration (Audio)**:
    *   *"Bring proactive operational intelligence and governed auto-healing to your Salesforce environment.*
    *   *Start your free trial of SentinelFlow + Zentom AI in a developer sandbox today."*
*   **Sync Timing**:
    *   `1:50` - Dashboard overview fade.
    *   `1:55` - Ending logo and AppExchange CTA screen.
