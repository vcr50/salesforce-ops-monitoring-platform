### 3.2 Observational Notes

The following observational notes were captured during the monitoring window. These are not issues but areas for potential proactive improvement:

| # | Observation | Area | Priority |
|---|-------------|------|----------|
| 1 | Prediction scoring weights may require recalibration under sustained high-volume production loads exceeding pilot simulation levels. | Prediction Engine | Low |
| 2 | CometD streaming sessions may timeout during very long-running operator sessions (8+ hours continuous). Exponential backoff reconnect (5 attempts) mitigates this, but a proactive keep-alive enhancement could strengthen resilience. | Streaming Telemetry | Low |
| 3 | Custom Metadata cost settings ($80.00/hr, $150.00/case) are static and may not reflect actual organizational costs over time. A quarterly review cadence should be formalized. | Cost Analytics | Low |
| 4 | At very high concurrent incident volumes, `SELECT FOR UPDATE` row locking could theoretically produce contention. The existing audit log fallback mitigates this, but preemptive query optimization may reduce lock hold times. | Auto-Heal | Low |

## 4. Patch Candidates

Based on the post-release findings and observational notes, the following are evaluated as potential patch candidates:

| # | Candidate | Type | Justification | Priority | Recommendation |
|---|-----------|------|---------------|----------|----------------|
| 1 | CometD keep-alive heartbeat enhancement | Stability | Proactive resilience improvement for long-running streaming sessions. Current exponential backoff already mitigates, but a heartbeat would prevent disconnection entirely. | Low | **DEFER** — Current mitigation is sufficient. Monitor for actual timeout reports. |
| 2 | Row lock hold time optimization | Performance | Reduce `SELECT FOR UPDATE` lock hold duration in `AutoHealExecutionService.cls` to minimize theoretical contention at high concurrency. | Low | **DEFER** — Zero lock errors observed in 7 days. Monitor for actual contention under production growth. |
| 3 | Apex governor limit headroom logging | Observability | Add telemetry logging for CPU time and DML headroom consumption percentages to proactively detect approaching limits before they cause failures. | Low | **CONSIDER** for v1.2.1 — Would improve observability without risk. |

**Assessment**: No patch candidates are urgent or blocking. All candidates are proactive improvements that can be evaluated for a future v1.2.1 maintenance release.
