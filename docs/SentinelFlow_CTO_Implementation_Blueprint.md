# SentinelFlow — CTO Implementation Blueprint

## Vision Statement

SentinelFlow is an AI-powered operational continuity platform designed to protect business workflows, revenue operations, and automation systems from failures across CRM, ERP, billing, support, and commerce ecosystems.

The long-term vision is to become:

> “The AI operational safety layer for revenue systems.”

SentinelFlow should not feel like traditional monitoring software.
It should feel like:

- intelligent
- autonomous
- calm
- simple
- trustworthy
- business-aware

Core philosophy:

> Complex backend. Extremely simple frontend.

# Strategic Product Direction

## Phase 1 — Salesforce-Native MVP

Goal:
Build fast, validate real customer pain, and establish operational continuity niche.

### Core MVP Modules

#### 1. Flow Shield
Detect failed Salesforce Flows.

#### 2. Apex Job Shield
Monitor Queueable, Batch, and Scheduled Apex jobs.

#### 3. Integration Health
Monitor APIs, Stripe, ERP systems, and webhook failures.

#### 4. Revenue Risk Engine
Map workflow failures to business impact.

Example:

> “This issue may affect ₹18L pipeline revenue.”

#### 5. AI Guardian UI
Simple AI-first operational interface.
