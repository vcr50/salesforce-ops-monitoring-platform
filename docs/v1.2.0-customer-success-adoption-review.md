# SentinelFlow v1.2.0 — Customer Success / Adoption Review

This document evaluates SentinelFlow v1.2.0 operator adoption, training coverage, platform value realization, and identifies any friction or blockers to sustained enterprise adoption.

---

## 1. Purpose

With v1.2.0 confirmed technically stable (Milestone 66 CLOSED), production-hardened (Milestone 67 CLOSED), and security/compliance-cleared (Milestone 68 CLOSED), this review shifts focus to the **human dimension** of platform success: are operators actively using the platform, is it delivering measurable business value, and is it positioned for long-term enterprise adoption?

> [!IMPORTANT]
> This review is **observational and qualitative**. No code changes are made. All findings are classified as healthy, monitor, or action-required.

### Review Objectives

1. Evaluate operator adoption metrics across all persona types
2. Assess training and onboarding material completeness
3. Measure platform value realization against projected outcomes (cost savings, MTTR)
4. Gather structured feedback from operator and admin personas
5. Identify adoption blockers and friction points
6. Define KPIs for sustained long-term adoption
7. Recommend actions to maximize enterprise customer value
