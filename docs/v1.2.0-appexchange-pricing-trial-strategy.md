# SentinelFlow + Zentom AI v1.2.0 - AppExchange Pricing / Trial Strategy

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the AppExchange pricing and trial strategy for SentinelFlow + Zentom AI v1.2.0 while Milestone 71C remains blocked by namespace and Managed 2GP setup. It supports non-blocked listing preparation and keeps all customer-facing pricing, trial, and ROI language compliant, conservative, and non-guaranteed.

## 2. Pricing model overview

SentinelFlow should be positioned as a paid enterprise operations product with three clear tiers: Starter, Professional, and Enterprise. The public AppExchange listing should avoid overcommitting to exact financial outcomes and should make final commercial terms subject to publisher quote, contract scope, environment count, data volume, and support needs.

Recommended listing posture:

- Paid app with free trial.
- Tiered subscription model.
- Starter for operational visibility.
- Professional for prediction and governed approval workflows.
- Enterprise for Governed Auto-Heal, compliance evidence, cost savings analytics, and executive reporting.
- Pricing finalized through publisher quote for production deployments.

## 3. Free trial strategy

Offer a guided free trial that lets buyers validate operational fit before production purchase. The trial should be suitable for a Salesforce sandbox or controlled trial org and should focus on visibility, explainability, and approval workflows rather than automated remediation.

Recommended trial structure:

- Trial length: 14 to 30 days.
- Trial environment: Sandbox, Trialforce org, or non-critical evaluation org.
- Included experience: Dashboards, sample incidents, incident visibility, audit logs, prediction examples, explainable warning cards, and Guardian Gate approval flows.
- Excluded by default: Production Governed Auto-Heal execution and high-risk remediation without explicit customer configuration.
- Buyer success metric: Estimated operational value from faster triage, clearer incident ownership, and better approval traceability.

## 4. Pilot / proof-of-value option

Offer a paid pilot or proof-of-value option for enterprise customers that need internal validation before a full subscription. The pilot should establish a baseline and then report estimated improvements using customer-approved metrics.

Recommended pilot structure:

- Duration: 30 to 60 days.
- Scope: One business unit, one Salesforce production-adjacent environment, or a defined incident category.
- Baseline metrics: Incident volume, mean time to resolution, manual approval cycle time, rework rate, and audit evidence preparation effort.
- Output: Executive summary showing Estimated MTTR reduction, Estimated cost savings, and Estimated operational value.
- Governance: Human approval required for high-risk actions throughout the pilot.

## 6. Starter tier

Starter should give teams a low-friction entry point for incident operations visibility.

Included capabilities:

- Monitoring.
- Dashboard visibility.
- Incident visibility.
- Audit logs.
- Basic status and severity views.
- Standard setup documentation.

Recommended positioning:

Starter is best for teams that need a reliable command-center view of operational health before adopting prediction or governed remediation workflows.

## 7. Professional tier

Professional should be positioned as the recommended tier for teams that want predictive operations with human-reviewed action controls.

Included capabilities:

- Everything in Starter.
- Zentom AI prediction engine.
- Explainable warning cards.
- Guardian Gate approvals.
- Approval queues and operator review flows.
- Expanded configuration support.

Recommended positioning:

Professional is best for teams that want Estimated MTTR reduction through earlier warnings, clearer prioritization, and governed approvals.

## 8. Enterprise tier

Enterprise should be positioned for regulated, executive-visible, or high-volume operations teams.

Included capabilities:

- Everything in Professional.
- Governed Auto-Heal.
- Compliance evidence.
- Cost savings analytics.
- Executive reports.
- Advanced rollout planning.
- Security, compliance, and architecture review support.

Recommended positioning:

Enterprise is best for organizations that need auditable automation with human approval required for high-risk actions, executive reporting, and compliance-ready evidence.

## 10. Estimated ROI wording rules

Approved wording:

- Estimated cost savings.
- Estimated MTTR reduction.
- Estimated operational value.
- Governed Auto-Heal.
- Human approval required for high-risk actions.

Avoided wording:

- Guaranteed ROI.
- Guaranteed savings.
- Fully autonomous critical remediation.
- Approval bypass.
- Unrestricted auto-healing.

All public copy, sales decks, demo scripts, and customer-facing proof-of-value summaries should describe outcomes as estimates based on observed customer environment data, configured workflows, and validated operational baselines.

## 11. AppExchange listing pricing text

Recommended listing text:

SentinelFlow + Zentom AI is available as a paid subscription with Starter, Professional, and Enterprise tiers. A guided free trial is available for sandbox or controlled evaluation environments. Production pricing depends on tier, deployment scope, environment count, data volume, and support requirements. Contact TomCodeX Inc. for a final quote and rollout plan.

Recommended trial text:

Start with a guided trial to evaluate monitoring, dashboards, incident visibility, explainable predictions, Guardian Gate approvals, and audit evidence workflows. Trial outcomes should be framed as Estimated MTTR reduction, Estimated cost savings, and Estimated operational value based on customer-specific baseline data.

Recommended Enterprise text:

Enterprise customers can add Governed Auto-Heal, compliance evidence, cost savings analytics, and executive reports. Human approval is required for high-risk actions.

## 12. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| Buyer interprets ROI language as guaranteed | Use only estimated wording and require baseline-dependent qualification |
| Buyer expects unrestricted automation | State that high-risk actions require human approval |
| Trial environment differs from production | Position trial results as directional and validate again during pilot |
| Pricing confusion across tiers | Keep tier capability boundaries clear and route final quotes through publisher review |
| 71C packaging remains blocked | Continue listing assets, security review prep, pricing strategy, and sales enablement without modifying package configuration |

## 13. Final recommendation

Use a paid tiered subscription model with a guided free trial and an optional paid pilot / proof-of-value path. Publish conservative AppExchange pricing language that emphasizes tier availability and trial access while routing final production pricing through TomCodeX Inc.

Keep all ROI and automation language non-guaranteed. Continue using Estimated cost savings, Estimated MTTR reduction, Estimated operational value, Governed Auto-Heal, and Human approval required for high-risk actions.

71C remains BLOCKED until namespace and Managed 2GP setup are resolved. No package configuration or `sfdx-project.json` changes are required for this milestone.
